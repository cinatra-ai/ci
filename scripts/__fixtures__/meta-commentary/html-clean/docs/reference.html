Reports are generated on demand from the records you select, and the run tells you when it lands. An approval landed while you were away.

Records you hand-edit in the connected app are re-read on the next sync. There is no need to hand-edit the field mappings; the connector maintains them.

See issue #123 for troubleshooting. If the webhook has not landed after five minutes, retry it.

The connector implements the ratified OAuth 2.1 specification, and the security policy was ratified by the standards committee.

Only ratified algorithms run in FIPS mode. Following the decision returned by the policy engine, the run either continues or stops.

Scheduled exports are not yet supported. Requests still in flight are allowed to complete during shutdown, and events yet to land remain queued.

The identifier 0xAC12 is a device address, not a criterion, and the colour #1607 is a palette entry.

Jump to overview.md#12, to docs/overview.md#12, to guides/setup.html#3 and to reference/deck.pdf#7 for the worked examples.

The publish decision is recorded on the run, the spec status of a connector is shown on its card, and the design notes for this integration are in the appendix.
